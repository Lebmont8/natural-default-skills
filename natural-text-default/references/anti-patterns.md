# Anti-patterns

These are soft editing signals, not forbidden tokens. A phrase may be correct when the context genuinely requires it.

## Structural signals

- generic opening before the actual point;
- automatic hook, three balanced sections, and summary;
- every paragraph ending with a mini-conclusion;
- several headings for a short message;
- bullets where ordinary prose would be easier;
- forced contrast patterns repeated across a text;
- closing that repeats the request instead of advancing it.

## Meaning signals

- one idea paraphrased two or three times;
- broad claims with no actor, object, time, evidence, or consequence;
- adjectives doing the work of facts;
- claims of uniqueness, importance, effectiveness, or transformation without support;
- invented urgency, confidence, emotion, familiarity, or personal experience;
- exhaustive background when the reader needs one action.

## Russian soft signals

Review, but do not automatically delete:

- «В современном мире…»
- «Важно отметить / следует подчеркнуть…»
- «Давайте разберёмся / погрузимся…»
- «является ключевым аспектом»
- «не просто X, а Y»
- «это не только X, но и Y»
- «комплексный подход»
- «уникальная возможность»
- «эффективное решение»
- «вывести на новый уровень»
- «в конечном итоге»
- «подводя итог»

Repeated em dashes, colons, participial constructions, and perfectly parallel lists can contribute to a synthetic rhythm, but none is inherently wrong in Russian.

## English soft signals

Review, but do not automatically delete:

- “In today's fast-paced world”
- “It is important to note”
- “Let's delve into”
- “a testament to”
- “plays a pivotal role”
- “not just X, but Y”
- “unlock / elevate / transform”
- “seamless / robust / cutting-edge”
- “in conclusion”
- “whether you're X or Y”

## Bad fixes

Do not humanize by:

- inserting random typos;
- adding slang unrelated to the sender;
- making every sentence fragmentary;
- exaggerating emotion;
- inventing anecdotes or opinions;
- optimizing for detector evasion;
- banning ordinary punctuation or every occurrence of a common phrase.
